# NFC Integration Strategy & Functional Requirements

Beyond simple attendance, NFC technology will serve as the **digital identity** for every student and staff member in the Ishuri Hub ecosystem.

## 1. Attendance & Tracking
- **Morning Arrival**: Gate entry logs official "Present" status (Impacts Report Cards).
- **Classroom Attendance**: Teacher taps phone to student card to confirm presence in specific periods (Granular tracking).
- **Event Participation**: Tracking attendance at mandatory school assemblies or sports events.

## 2. Financial Ecosystem (Cashless Campus)
- **Canteen/Tuck Shop**: Students pay for lunch/snacks by tapping their card. Parents preload wallets via Mobile Money.
    - *Benefit*: Eliminates cash handling/theft, ensures money is spent on food.
- **School Fees**: Verification of payment status at the Bursar's office.

## 3. Access Control & Security
- **Restricted Areas**: Staff-only access to Server Rooms, Exam Strongrooms, or Staff Rooms.
- **Dormitory Access**: Ensuring only boarders enter specific blocks.
- **Visitor Management**: Temporary NFC cards for visitors tracked throughout the campus.

## 4. Academic & Library Resources
- **Library Loans**: "Tap-to-Borrow". Faster checkout/return of books.
- **Exam Entry**: Verifying student identity before National Exams to prevent impersonation.
- **Lab Equipment**: checking out expensive equipment (tablets, physics tools) to specific students.

## 5. Transport (School Bus)
- **Boarding/Alighting**: Students tap when entering/leaving the bus.
- **Parent Notifications**: Auto-SMS to parents: *"Your child has boarded Bus A"* / *"Your child has arrived at school"*.

## 6. Health & Safety
- **Medical Records**: School Nurse taps card to pull up allergy info and medical history instantly during emergencies.
