Empowering Entrepreneurs Through Action

VNR runs several high-impact programs designed to serve entrepreneurs at various stages of growth, from ideation to scale. Each program is carefully crafted to address specific needs and challenges faced by Rwanda's emerging business leaders.

The Business Shower Program

An intensive 8-week mentorship and business-preparation program designed to help entrepreneurs refine, structure, and showcase their ventures for potential seed funding and investment.

  • Weekly mentorship sessions
  • Business model refinement
  • Pitch preparation & coaching
  • Investor networking opportunities
  • Seed funding showcase event
Apply Now

Business Formalization Support Desk

A dedicated system that guides entrepreneurs through legal registration, RDB/RRA compliance, and establishing proper corporate governance for formal operation.

  • RDB business registration support
  • Tax compliance guidance (RRA)
  • Licensing & permits assistance
  • Corporate governance setup
  • Legal documentation support
Get Started

Community Incubation Pods

Local clusters offering group mentorship, financial training, and sector-specific coaching to support growth and collaboration at the grassroots level.

  • Community-based support groups
  • Peer learning & networking
  • Financial literacy training
  • Sector-specific mentorship
  • Resource sharing platforms
Join a Pod

SME Growth Clinics

Tailor-made advisory sessions for Small and Medium Enterprises ready to expand operations, enter new markets, and seek significant scaling opportunities.

  • One-on-one strategic advisory
  • Market expansion strategies
  • Operational efficiency consulting
  • Investment readiness preparation
  • Growth acceleration support
