About Soledicom

Dedicated to natural beauty and wellness

Who We Are

Soledicom Company Ltd is a Rwandan company dedicated to developing plant-based cosmetic products under the trademark SANESOL. Founded in March 2022, we operate from the Masaka Business Incubation Center.

Our Founder and Managing Director, Dr. Marie-Solange Uwineza, holds a PhD in Applied Chemistry and Environment. With a passion for aromatic and medicinal plants explored during her doctoral research, she returned to Rwanda to create effective, natural solutions for hair and skin care.
