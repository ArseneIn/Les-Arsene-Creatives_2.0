Frequently Asked Questions

Is my donation tax-deductible? expand_more
Yes, AKWOS is a registered non-profit organization. Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law in your country of residence. You will receive an official tax receipt immediately via email.
How is my money used? expand_more
We pride ourselves on efficiency. 85% of every dollar goes directly to our sports and education programs for women and girls. The remaining 15% covers essential administrative and fundraising costs to keep our mission running.
Can I donate in a currency other than USD? expand_more
Yes! Our secure payment processor automatically handles currency conversion at the current exchange rate. You can donate using any major credit card from anywhere in the world.
